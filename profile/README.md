### Who We Are

A manufacturers' representative for test and measurement instrumentation, serving
the **Great Lakes to Mid-Atlantic** region. We pair engineers with the right
instrument &mdash; and when the catalog doesn't have it, we build it.

### What We Cover

**Test &amp; Measurement** &mdash; Instrumentation across every discipline, from bench to production floor.

**Sensors &amp; Transducers** &mdash; Force, pressure, torque, temperature, displacement, and current measurement.

**Power Supplies &amp; Analyzers** &mdash; Programmable DC and AC sources, power quality analysis, and grid simulation.

**Data Acquisition** &mdash; Portable and distributed DAQ for test cells, vehicles, and the field.

**Calibration &amp; Standards** &mdash; Pressure, temperature, and electrical standards with traceable calibration.

**MiTech Custom Solutions** &mdash; Engineered-to-order systems when off-the-shelf doesn't fit.
